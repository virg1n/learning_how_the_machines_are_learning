**One Word: Indicator of correlation**
**Short:** If they are **correlated**, dot product of them will be **positive**
If they are **perpandicular**, dot product of them will be **0**
If they are **NOT correlated**, dot product of them will be **negative**

Why?
1) x1​v1​+x2​v2​+⋯
		In this formula you are checking, dimension by dimension, whether the two vectors “agree” on their direction. If one is positive and the other is negative, they’re voting oppositely. If they are VERY positive -> bigger dot product for this dimension. Then - add all agreements.
2) formula = ∣x∣ ∣y∣ ∣cosθ∣, where cosθ is can be 1 iff they are looking in 1 direction

Formula:
$$
\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = a_1 b_1 + a_2 b_2 + a_3 b_3.
$$
$$
\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = \|\mathbf{a}\| \|\mathbf{b}\| \cos\theta.
$$
